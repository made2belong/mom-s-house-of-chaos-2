# Mom's House of Chaos

![A colorful cartoon living room filled with household messes, where a detective mom investigates five mischievous children.](cover.png)

Mom's House of Chaos is a funny household mystery game for one player who enjoys solving clues and catching fictional troublemakers.

## Play it

Game link: [Play Mom's House of Chaos](ADD-YOUR-GITHUB-PAGES-URL-HERE)

You need a phone or computer and one player.

## How to play

1. Read the household mystery.
2. Tap objects around the room to find clues.
3. Review the five fictional child suspects.
4. Accuse the child you think caused the mess.
5. Earn 10 points for a correct first guess or 5 for a correct second guess.
6. Solve all five mysteries, see your score, and play again.
